## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing one year of U.S. e-commerce sales transaction data from **2019** using Python and data analysis and visualization techniques.

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful visualizations, identify important business trends, and generate actionable business insights.

# 🧠 Key Business Insights

### 1. 📅 December is the Best Sales Month

December generated approximately **$4.6M** in revenue, indicating strong holiday-season demand.

### 2. 🌎 San Francisco Leads in Orders

San Francisco recorded approximately **44,858 orders**, making it the strongest city by order volume.

### 3. 🕐 Advertising Opportunities

The analysis identified **11 AM and 7 PM** as important ordering periods.

### 4. 💻 MacBook Pro Leads Revenue

The MacBook Pro Laptop generated approximately **$8M** in revenue.

### 5. 🔌 USB-C Charging Cable Leads Unit Sales

The USB-C Charging Cable sold approximately **23,975 units**, making it the highest-volume product.

### 6. 📦 Electronics Dominate Revenue

Electronics contributed approximately **78%** of total revenue, while Accessories contributed approximately **15%**.

### 7. 📈 Q4 is a Strong Sales Period

October through December contributed approximately **35%** of annual revenue.

---

# 🎯 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

### 📅 1. Start Holiday Promotions Early

Since Q4 contributes a significant share of annual revenue, holiday promotions can begin in **October** rather than waiting until December.

### 📦 2. Use Product Bundling

High-volume products such as charging cables can be bundled with higher-value electronics to encourage cross-selling.

### 🏭 3. Prepare Inventory for Q4

Inventory and logistics planning should be strengthened before the Q4 demand increase.

### 🕐 4. Optimize Advertisement Timing

Advertising campaigns can focus around the identified high-order periods of **11 AM and 7 PM**.

### 🌎 5. Focus on High-Order Cities

Cities with strong order volumes can receive additional marketing and logistics support.
