## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **MainFlow Internship — Project 1, Task 2: Advanced Visualisation & Business Insights**.

The project analyzes one full year of **U.S. e-commerce sales transaction data from 2019** using Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques.

The main objective is to transform raw sales data into meaningful visualizations and business insights that can support better decisions related to:

- Sales performance
- Customer demand
- Product performance
- City-level orders
- Advertisement timing
- Product categories
- Pricing and quantity relationships
- Seasonal trends
- Business recommendations

---

## 🎯 Task 2 Objectives

The major objectives of this analysis were:

1. Analyze monthly sales revenue.
2. Identify cities with the highest number of orders.
3. Determine the best hours for advertising.
4. Identify top products by revenue.
5. Analyze monthly revenue by product category.
6. Study the relationship between product price and quantity sold.
7. Analyze top products by quantity sold.
8. Examine sales performance by city.
9. Analyze sales by hour.
10. Analyze sales by day of the month.
11. Perform correlation analysis.
12. Generate descriptive statistics.
13. Extract meaningful business insights.
14. Provide actionable business recommendations.

💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. 🎄 Prepare Early for Q4

Since Q4 contributes approximately 35% of annual revenue, inventory and promotional planning should begin before the holiday season.

2. 📢 Optimize Advertisement Timing

Advertising can be focused around the identified peak ordering periods:

11 AM and 7 PM

3. 📦 Create Product Bundles

High-volume products such as charging cables can be bundled with higher-value electronics.

This can encourage cross-selling and increase the value of individual purchases.

4. 🏬 Strengthen Inventory Planning

The strong Q4 performance indicates the importance of preparing sufficient inventory before the holiday shopping period.

5. 🌎 Focus on High-Order Cities

Cities with high order volumes can receive additional attention in areas such as:

Inventory availability

Delivery capacity

Regional promotions

Customer support

Logistics planning



---

🔍 Key Business Takeaway

The analysis demonstrates an important distinction between sales volume and revenue generation.

Low-priced products such as charging cables can generate very high unit volumes, while high-priced products such as laptops can contribute substantially to total revenue.

Therefore, an effective e-commerce strategy should balance:

High-Volume Products
        +
High-Revenue Products
        +
Seasonal Demand
        +
Regional Demand
        +
Optimized Advertising
        =
Better Business Decisions
