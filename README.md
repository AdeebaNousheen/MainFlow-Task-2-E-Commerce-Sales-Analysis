## Internship / Project Overview

As part of the **Main Flow Internship**, this project covers **Project 1 — Task 2: Advanced Visualisation & Business Insights**.

The task focuses on using data visualization techniques to understand sales performance, customer ordering patterns, product performance, category-level trends, and relationships between important numerical variables.

# Key Findings

The Task 2 analysis produced the following key findings:

- **December** was the best sales month — approximately **$4.6M**
- **San Francisco** was the top city — approximately **44,858 orders**
- The best advertisement times were **11 AM and 7 PM**
- **MacBook Pro** generated the highest revenue — approximately **$8M**
- **USB-C Charging Cable** sold the most units — **23,975 units**
- **Electronics** accounted for approximately **78% of revenue**
- **Accessories** accounted for approximately **15% of revenue**
- **Q4 (October–December)** contributed approximately **35% of annual revenue**

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

1. **Start holiday promotions in October rather than waiting until December.**

2. **Bundle high-volume low-cost products**, such as charging cables, with expensive electronics.

3. **Prepare inventory early for Q4 demand** to support the stronger sales period.

4. **Focus advertising around 11 AM and 7 PM**, based on the identified peak ordering periods.

5. **Consider stronger logistics and warehouse support in high-order cities** to support regional demand.
