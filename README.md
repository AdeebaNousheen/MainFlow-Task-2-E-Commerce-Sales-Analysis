# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs an end-to-end analysis of an e-commerce sales dataset using Python.

The objective is to identify sales trends, top-performing products and cities, customer ordering patterns, product category performance, and important business insights.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab

## 📊 Analysis Performed

- Data cleaning and preprocessing
- Monthly sales revenue analysis
- Top-selling products analysis
- Sales by city
- Sales by hour
- Sales by day of the week
- Quantity sold by product
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Monthly sales growth
- Correlation analysis
- Sales distribution
- Product category analysis
- Monthly revenue by product category
- Price vs. quantity sold analysis
- Business insights and recommendations

## 📈 Visualizations

The project includes:

1. Monthly Sales Revenue
2. Top 10 Products by Sales
3. Sales by Product Category
4. Monthly Revenue by Product Category Heatmap
5. Sales by City
6. Sales by Hour
7. Sales by Day
8. Price vs. Quantity Sold

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis identifies:

- Highest-performing sales months
- Best-performing cities
- Top products by revenue
- Products with the highest quantity sold
- Peak sales hours
- Highest-performing days
- Category-wise monthly revenue trends
- Relationship between product price and quantity sold

## ▶️ How to Run

1. Open the Python file in Google Colab.
2. Mount Google Drive.
3. Provide the required e-commerce sales dataset.
4. Run the analysis cells sequentially.
5. View the generated visualizations and business insights.
